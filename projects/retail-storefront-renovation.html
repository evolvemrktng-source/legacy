Retail Storefront Renovation

City: Troy, MI Category: Commercial Completed: 2024

Home / Projects / Retail Storefront Renovation

Project Goal

Modernize a dated retail storefront’s facade and interior layout for a new tenant.

Existing Condition

An outdated storefront facade and an interior layout that didn’t reflect the new tenant’s merchandising needs.

Challenges

Updating the exterior facade required coordination with the shopping center’s landlord and design guidelines, while the interior renovation had to work around existing structural columns.

Scope

Architectural design for facade and interior renovation, engineering coordination for structural elements, and construction.

Design Solution

A refreshed facade that met the landlord’s design criteria while giving the tenant stronger street presence, paired with an interior layout that worked with the existing column grid instead of against it.

Construction Solution

Renovation phased to allow continued access to neighboring tenants in the shopping center throughout construction.

Final Outcome

A modernized storefront that gave the tenant improved visibility and a layout suited to their retail operations.
