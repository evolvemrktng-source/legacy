Custom Luxury Home

City: Bloomfield Hills, MI Category: Residential Completed: 2025

Home / Projects / Custom Luxury Home

Project Goal

Deliver a complete custom home on a vacant lot — from architectural concept through construction and move-in — without the client needing to manage separate architecture, engineering, and construction firms.

Existing Condition

A vacant, wooded parcel with no existing structures, requiring full site planning, utility coordination, and municipal approval from the ground up.

Challenges

Sloped topography and municipal setback requirements shaped the buildable footprint, while the client’s timeline required design, engineering, and permitting to move in parallel rather than in sequence.

Scope

Architectural design, structural and MEP engineering, permitting, and full construction of a single-family custom residence, including site work and landscaping coordination.

Design Solution

An architectural plan that worked with the site’s natural grade rather than against it, reducing excavation cost while preserving mature trees along the property line.

Construction Solution

A sequenced construction schedule that coordinated foundation, framing, and MEP rough-in with municipal inspections to avoid delays, with weekly progress updates to the client throughout.

Final Outcome

A completed custom home delivered on budget, with the client’s architecture, engineering, and construction handled by a single accountable team from concept through occupancy.
