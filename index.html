About Legacy Builds Group

Built Different. Built Better.

Legacy Builds Group is a full-service design-build company serving residential, commercial, and industrial clients throughout Metro Detroit. We manage architecture, engineering, MEP systems, permitting, construction, and project coordination through one integrated team.

Our structure reduces handoffs, improves communication, and allows design decisions to be evaluated with real construction knowledge from the beginning.
